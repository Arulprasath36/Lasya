About Madhu Dance Academy

A place to belong, grow, and shine.

Whether you are a parent searching for a welcoming place where your child can build confidence, or an adult ready to move, reconnect with dance, and do something joyful for yourself, Madhu Dance Academy is a place where you can feel at home.

Our Bollywood dance classes offer more than choreography. Young dancers learn to express themselves, make meaningful friendships, and feel proud of their progress. Adult dancers find an energizing space to stay active, let go of everyday stress, connect with others, and rediscover the confidence that comes from moving to music.

You do not need previous dance experience or the confidence to perform before you begin. We meet every dancer where they are and help them grow at their own pace—with patience, encouragement, and genuine care. Because everyone deserves a place where they feel supported, celebrated, and free to shine.

For Young Dancers

A patient and encouraging environment where children can be themselves, make friends, build confidence, and feel proud of every step forward.

For Adult Dancers

An uplifting space to move, stay active, release everyday stress, and reconnect with the joy of dancing—while being part of a supportive group.

Come As You Are

No previous experience and no pressure to be perfect. Wherever you are starting from, you will be welcomed, supported, and encouraged to grow at your own pace.

Meet the Founder

Mathumitha (Madhu) Balu

For Mathumitha (Madhu) Balu, dance has always been more than movement—it's a way to build confidence, celebrate creativity, and bring people together. Through Madhu Dance Academy, she has created a welcoming space where children, teens, and adults can learn, perform, and discover the joy of dance.

Although trained in biotechnology, Madhu's passion for dance began in her school and college years, where she earned recognition through numerous dance competitions. What started as a lifelong passion has grown into a mission of inspiring others through movement and performance.

For more than eight years, Madhu has choreographed and taught students across the United States, helping beginners take their first steps while guiding experienced dancers toward confident performances. Her students and teams have received recognition at Indian cultural events, reflecting her dedication to excellence in choreography, performance, and teaching.

Her signature style blends the vibrant energy of Bollywood with the elegance of South Indian cinematic dance, creating classes that are expressive, energetic, and welcoming for dancers of every age and experience level.

As a Certified Zumba Instructor and designer of a dance fitness app, Madhu also brings a strong focus on health, movement, and joyful fitness, encouraging students to stay active while enjoying every class.

“Whether it's a child's first dance class, a teen preparing for the spotlight, or an adult taking a break from everyday routines, I believe every dancer deserves a place to learn, grow, and shine. That's my vision for Madhu Dance Academy.”
— Mathumitha (Madhu) Balu
